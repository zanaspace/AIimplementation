Meeting Pre-Read Generator

When a meeting is scheduled, CICOD-AI reads the title, agenda and attendees and finds the relevant recent context in Drive, ECMS, InMail and earlier minutes. It drafts a Pre-Read Brief covering the purpose, agenda, background with sources, open action items from the last meeting and the decisions needed, plus what each attendee should prepare. The brief is sent by InMail 24 hours before the meeting, and each copy only includes items that attendee is allowed to open.

Screen: Conference → Room(s) → Schedule MeetingComponent: <ai-meeting-preread>Endpoint: /conference/preread

2. How it works

Search finds the context and an LLM writes the brief. The access check runs for every attendee, not just the organiser.

Read the meeting

The title, agenda lines, attendees and start time are sent to /conference/preread when the organiser asks.

Find context

Hybrid search (keyword and embeddings) over the unified index covers Drive files, ECMS memos and queue figures, InMail circulars, and minutes from earlier meetings with the same attendees. The top 6–8 sources from the last 90 days are kept.

Check access for each attendee

Every source is checked against each attendee's Drive access, ECMS role and the file's classification. Items an attendee can't open are left out of their copy, and the organiser is told which ones.

Write the brief

An LLM on the sovereign model writes the purpose, background (each point cites its source), decisions needed and a "prepare" list for each attendee. Open action items come from the last meeting's minutes.

Review & schedule

The organiser can untick sources and edit any text. Scheduling queues an InMail for 24 hours before the start. If the meeting is sooner, it's sent straight away.

Learn

Excluded sources, edits and open rates are sent to /feedback. Sources organisers keep removing are ranked lower next time.

3. Benefits to this feature

These are targets. The baselines come from Conference attendance and meeting-minute action items before the pilot.

−15 min

Shorter meetings

Less time spent at the start of the meeting catching everyone up. Attendees arrive having read the background.

0 min

Organiser prep time

No more hunting through Drive and ECMS for the right files. The brief takes one click and a quick review.

+50%

Action items closed

Open items from the last meeting are put in front of their owners 24 hours before, with overdue ones highlighted.

No leaks

Classification respected

Secret and Confidential files reach only the attendees cleared for them, so the organiser can't overshare by accident.

Decisions

Meetings that decide

Listing the decisions needed up front makes it clearer what the meeting must conclude. This feeds the AI Minutes of Meeting.

4. How to implement it in Conference

Mount (Schedule Meeting)

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ai/components/meeting-preread.css">
<script src="/ai/shared/ai-gateway.js"></script>
<script src="/ai/components/meeting-preread.js"></script>

<ai-meeting-preread source="#schedule-form"
                    lead-hours="24"></ai-meeting-preread>

<script>
el.addEventListener('ai-preread-schedule', e => {
  // one InMail per attendee, with only their permitted attachments
  e.detail.perAttendee.forEach(p => inmail.schedule({
    to: p.name, at: e.detail.sendAt, subject: e.detail.subject,
    body: e.detail.brief, prepare: p.prepare,
    attachments: e.detail.sources.filter(s => !p.withheld.includes(s.id))
  }));
});
</script>

Build steps

  1. Add an agenda field to Schedule Meeting (one item per line) and store it on the meeting.
  2. Make sure the unified index covers Drive, ECMS memos and tasks, InMail circulars and meeting minutes, with access metadata.
  3. Build /conference/preread: hybrid search, then an access check for each attendee, then the LLM brief on the sovereign model.
  4. Add scheduled sending to InMail (a queued message with a send time), with an access re-check just before sending.
  5. Show scheduled pre-reads in Invites History so they can be edited or cancelled.
  6. Pilot on management meetings for one month. Track open rate, time spent on the first item and how many action items are closed.

Controls

  • Feature flag ai.cicod.meetingPreread, set per tenant. The lead time (default 24 hours) is a tenant setting.
  • Access is checked for every attendee, twice: when the brief is drafted and again just before it's sent.
  • Nothing is sent without the organiser clicking Schedule. Scheduled pre-reads can be edited or cancelled until they're sent.
  • External attendees (outside the MDA) only get Official items, and only if the organiser ticks "include external".
  • Each brief, source decision and send goes to the Conference audit trail with the model version.
