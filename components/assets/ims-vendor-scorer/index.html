Vendor Performance Scorer

When an officer raises a purchase order, CICOD-AI scores each supplier's reliability for this order from their record in IMS: on-time delivery, quantity accepted by I&QA, invoice prices against the agreed price, price against other suppliers, and experience with the asset category. It recommends a supplier with a written justification for the file, and flags risks such as invoices split to stay under an approval limit. The officer makes the award decision.

Screen: Assets → Supplier → New Purchase OrderComponent: <ai-vendor-scorer>Endpoint: /ims/vendors/score

2. How it works

The score is a transparent weighted formula over IMS records, so every point can be traced to a delivery. The LLM only writes the justification.

Collect the record

For each supplier, the gateway reads every receipt: the promised date on the PO, the date received, Qty. Supplied / Approved / Rejected from I&QA, and the invoiced unit price against the agreed price in Payables.

Protect

Only supplier names and transaction figures are processed. Contact persons and bank details are never sent. Everything runs on the sovereign model.

Score

Five measures go from 0 to 1: on time (within a 2-day grace period), quantity accepted, invoice match (within 1%), price against the median for the same items, and category experience. They're combined with the officer's weights. A supplier new to the category loses 20%.

Check for risk

Rules look for same-day invoices each just under the approval limit (possible split orders), price outliers, chronic short delivery and repeated lateness. A split-invoice flag takes 15 points off and blocks the supplier from being recommended.

Recommend

The top eligible supplier is recommended. An LLM writes a short justification comparing it with the runner-up, and notes the delivery-date risk if the order is urgent.

Decide & learn

The officer picks any supplier. If it isn't the recommended one, a reason is needed. The choice and the justification are saved on the PO and sent to /feedback.

3. Benefits to this feature

These are targets. The baselines come from Payables and Received Asset History before the pilot.

−30%

Late deliveries

Orders go to suppliers who deliver on time, not just the ones who come to mind first.

Fewer rejects

Less I&QA rework

Suppliers with repeated short or rejected quantities drop down the list before the next order.

Flagged

Split-invoice detection

Orders split to stay under the approval limit are flagged automatically. This supports the audit of approval-threshold evasion.

100%

Justified awards

Every PO carries a written, evidence-based reason for the supplier choice, ready for internal audit and the Bureau of Public Procurement.

Price

Better value

Invoice prices above the agreed price, and prices above the market median, are visible before the award rather than after payment.

4. How to implement it in Assets (IMS)

Mount (New Purchase Order)

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ai/components/ims-vendor-scorer.css">
<script src="/ai/shared/ai-gateway.js"></script>
<script src="/ai/components/ims-vendor-scorer.js"></script>

<ai-vendor-scorer source="#po-form"
                  vendors="#vendor-table"></ai-vendor-scorer>

<script>
el.addEventListener('ai-vendor-select', e => {
  po.set('supplier', e.detail.vendor);
  po.set('justification', e.detail.justification);
  po.set('aiScore', e.detail.score);   // saved for approvers
});
el.addEventListener('ai-vendor-scores', e =>
  supplierTable.showScores(e.detail.scores));
</script>

Build steps

  1. Make sure each receipt stores the promised delivery date and the agreed unit price from the PO. Payables already has supplied, approved and rejected quantities and the invoiced price.
  2. Normalise supplier names (one supplier record, many spellings) with the same entity matcher used by Contact Dedup.
  3. Build /ims/vendors/score: scoring SQL/view, then risk rules, then an LLM justification on the sovereign model.
  4. Add the component to the New PO form and the score column to the Suppliers list. Store the score and justification on the PO.
  5. Show the score and justification on the Approval screen for every approval level.
  6. Pilot with IT and stationery purchases for one quarter. Compare late deliveries and I&QA rejections with the previous quarter.

Controls

  • Feature flag ai.assets.vendorScorer, set per tenant. The default weights and the approval limit used by the split-invoice rule are tenant settings.
  • It's a recommendation only. Choosing a different supplier is allowed, but a reason is needed, and both are kept on the PO.
  • A split-invoice flag also sends a notice to the Head of Procurement and is written to the IMS Audit log.
  • Scores are internal procurement records. They're never shown to suppliers and never used as the only award criterion.
  • Every scoring run, weight change and selection goes to the audit log with the model version.
