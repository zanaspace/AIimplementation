Based on the extensive suite of 28 AI modules we've built so far across ECMS, Drive, Assets, InMail, and Workspace, the system is already looking extremely robust!

However, there are still a few high-value areas in the CICOD suite where AI could have a massive impact. Here are some brand new module suggestions we could build prototypes for next:

1. PMS (Performance Management)
Note: We have an entry for the Appraisal Copilot in our registry, but we haven't actually built the prototype page for it yet!

Appraisal Copilot (Drafting): When it's time for annual reviews, managers stare at blank screens. This AI scans the user's closed tasks, memos, and meeting attendance over the last year and automatically drafts a performance review with concrete evidence.
Goal Alignment Assistant: When an employee drafts their SMART goals for the quarter, the AI checks them against the overarching Departmental Goals and suggests tweaks to ensure they are perfectly aligned.
2. IMS / Asset Management
Predictive Maintenance Auto-Scheduler: The AI analyzes historical repair tasks (e.g., generator maintenance, vehicle brake fixes) and predicts when an asset is likely to fail before it happens, automatically drafting a proactive repair task.
Vendor Performance Scorer: When trying to award a contract or purchase order, the AI analyzes past deliveries from vendors (checking for late deliveries or invoice discrepancies) and gives each vendor a reliability score to help make the decision.
3. Conference
Meeting Pre-Read Generator: When a meeting is scheduled, the AI looks at the title and the attendees, searches Drive/ECMS for relevant recent context, and automatically emails a "Pre-Read Brief" to all attendees 24 hours before the meeting so everyone is prepared.
4. Service Portal (Citizen Facing)
Public Sentiment & Feedback Analyzer: The AI reads all incoming citizen support tickets and feedback forms. At the end of the week, it generates an Executive Sentiment Report highlighting systemic issues (e.g., "70% of complaints this week were about the online payment gateway failing").
5. ECMS (Resource Management)
Shift & Roster Copilot: For departments managing shifts (like Customer Service or Security), the AI predicts peak busy times based on historical ticket volume and automatically generates the optimal staff roster to ensure the department is never understaffed.
