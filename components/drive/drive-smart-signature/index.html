Smart Signature & Routing

The Smart Signature Copilot transforms the document signing process. When you upload a file for signature, it automatically reads the document to identify who needs to sign it, matches them to users in CICOD, and maps out the exact locations for their signatures and dates. It also generates a "Signer's Brief"—a quick, plain-English summary of the financial commitments or obligations the user is about to authorize.

2. How it works

  • Signatory Extraction: The LLM reads the document looking for signature blocks, titles, and names, matching them against the tenant's user directory via the CICOD AI Gateway.
  • Auto-Placement: Using layout analysis, the AI returns bounding boxes for where the signature, initials, and date fields should be visually overlaid on the PDF.
  • Signer's Brief: A strict prompt analyzes the document specifically for financial amounts, legal obligations, and effective dates, presenting them to the signer so they know what they are authorizing without reading a 50-page contract.
