2. How it works

The AI assists you at every step of writing a memo without taking over control.

Capture

You provide rough bullet points, your role, and your department.

Protect

Sensitive data like phone numbers and emails are automatically protected before processing. Highly classified memos are kept strictly within secure environments.

Draft

The AI writes the memo in the proper official format, including the reference number, date, subject, structured paragraphs, and the correct phrasing for recommendations. Any monetary amounts are automatically spelled out in both figures and words.

Rewrite

You can instantly rephrase any sentence you write. Choose to make it sound more formal, more concise, address it to the Honorable Minister, or just fix spelling and grammar mistakes.

Classify

As you type, the AI reads your content and suggests whether the memo should be classified as Official, Confidential, or Secret, helping you catch sensitive information early.

Confirm & Learn

The AI never changes your memo without your permission. You review every draft or rewrite and must click to insert it. Your edits help the system learn and improve future suggestions.

4. How to implement it in ECMS

Mount (Create Memo page)

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ai/components/memo-copilot.css">
<script src="/ai/shared/ai-gateway.js"></script>
<script src="/ai/components/memo-copilot.js"></script>

<ai-memo-copilot editor="#memo-body"
                 from="Director (ICT)"
                 mda="CICOD"></ai-memo-copilot>

<script>
// the editor stays owned by the host page
on('ai-memo-insert', e => editor.setContent(e.detail.html, e.detail.mode));
on('ai-memo-replace-selection', e => editor.replaceRange(e.detail.range, e.detail.text));
on('ai-memo-classify', e => memoSettings.set('classification', e.detail.level));
</script>

Build steps

  1. Collect 3–5 approved memo templates per MDA (letterhead, Ref No pattern, sign-off) and store them in Memo Settings.
  2. Write the style guide prompt from the enterprise Rules and Financial Regulations; add number-to-words and Ref No generation as code.
  3. Expose /ecms/memo/draft, /rewrite and /classify on the CICOD-AI Gateway.
  4. Mount the panel in the Create Memo editor and wire the three events to the editor API and Settings.
  5. Pilot with two departments; track how much of each draft is kept unchanged at publish.

Controls

  • Feature flag ai.ecms.memoCopilot in ECMS Settings, on/off per tenant.
  • The classification chip only suggests. Publishing a Secret memo still needs the officer's own choice in Settings.
  • Every draft, rewrite and classification decision is written to the ECMS Audit Log (feature: CICOD-AI Memo).
  • The "Through" chain is only suggested from roles the author can address; drafts never include content from files the author can't open.
