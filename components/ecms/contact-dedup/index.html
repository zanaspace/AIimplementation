Contact Dedup & 360 Summary

This component automatically scans the contact list to find duplicates and junk entries. It proposes merges with pre-selected surviving values, cleans up invalid records, and provides a quick contact 360 summary with an inferred lifecycle stage.

Screen: ECMS → ContactsComponent: <ai-contact-insights>Endpoint: /ecms/contacts/quality · /summary

2. How it works

Deterministic matching finds the candidates, an LLM judge settles the borderline pairs and writes the reasons, and the officer makes every merge.

Capture

Contact names, created dates, lifecycle stage and hashed email and phone values are sent to /ecms/contacts/quality. The 360 card sends the contact ID only.

Protect

Emails and phone numbers never leave ECMS in clear text: the gateway compares salted hashes and shows masked values (t***@gmail.com, +234•••6456). Processing stays on the sovereign model.

Match

Blocking on surname and phone, then edit distance on names (≤ 2 letters) and exact hash matches. An LLM judge reviews borderline pairs. Junk is scored by keyboard-mash patterns, test keywords and zero activity.

Summarise

For the 360 card, the contact's tasks, emails and portal visits are summarised into requests, complaints, topic, last contact and sentiment, and rules infer the lifecycle stage.

Output

A banner with counts, a side-by-side merge view with surviving values pre-picked, a junk list with reasons, and suggested field fixes.

Confirm & learn

The officer merges, changes a surviving value, or marks "Not duplicates". Merged records are archived for 30 days. Every decision goes to /feedback and tunes the match threshold.

Build steps

  1. Add a merge API to ECMS Contacts that moves tasks, notes and history to the survivor and archives the others.
  2. Store salted hashes of normalised email and phone for matching.
  3. Run the scan nightly and on Add Contact; expose /ecms/contacts/quality on the CICOD-AI Gateway.
  4. Add /ecms/contacts/summary over the contact's tasks, InMail threads and portal engagements.
  5. Pilot with contact managers: start with groups above 90% confidence, then lower the threshold as acceptance allows.

Controls

  • Feature flag ai.ecms.contactDedup in ECMS Settings, on/off per tenant.
  • Groups below 75% confidence are not shown. Auto-merge is never enabled; a person always confirms.
  • Every merge, archive and stage change is written to the ECMS Audit Log (feature: CICOD-AI Contacts) and can be undone for 30 days.
  • Only users with the contact-manager permission can merge or archive. The 360 card only uses tasks the viewer can see.
