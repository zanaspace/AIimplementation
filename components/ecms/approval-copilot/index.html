Approval Copilot

Provides a quick summary for pending approvals, including request details, a policy check, anomalies, and a recommended decision. Safe bulk approve selects only low-risk items, leaving the final decision to the approver.

Screen: ECMS → Tasks → Task ApprovalsComponent: <ai-approval-copilot>Endpoints: /ecms/approval-digest · /ecms/approval-bulk-screen

2. How it works

Hard rules do the checks, retrieval finds the policy text, and an LLM writes the rationale. The recommendation always shows the rule and document behind it.

Capture

The approval request, its form fields and amount, attachments, the requester's history (prior approvals, outstanding advances), the budget line, and the approver's limit from Settings → Asset Thresholds.

Protect

The gateway removes personal data (NIN, account numbers, phone) and applies the classification of any attached file. Secret files are summarised only on the sovereign model.

Check rules

Deterministic checks: approval limit, budget left, unretired cash advance, split purchases (same vendor, short gap, combined above the limit), unit price against the Asset Registry, and missing documents.

Retrieve policy

The matching clauses are retrieved from Drive → General Documents → POLICY_DOCUMENTS (Financial Regulations, Stores procedure) and cited in the digest.

Recommend

An LLM writes the recommended decision and a short rationale from the check results. Bulk screening includes only items with no failed or flagged checks.

Confirm & learn

The approver accepts, overrides, or unticks bulk items. Overrides are logged via /feedback and reviewed each month to tune rules and prompts.

3. Benefits to this feature

Percentages are targets. Thresholds in the demo are illustrative. Production reads them from each tenant's Asset Thresholds settings.

−50%

Approval cycle time

Target. The approver reads one digest instead of opening every attachment and checking limits by hand.

4 of 9 in 1 click

Safe bulk approval

Routine, low-value items are approved together. Anything with a flag is left out, with the reason given.

100%

Policy checks on every item

Target. Every approval is checked against the limit, the budget, unretired advances and split purchases before it is signed.

Fewer audit queries

Better approval quality

Split purchases and unretired advances are caught before approval instead of at audit.

Cited

Decisions you can defend

Every recommendation names the Financial Regulations clause it relies on. The comment is saved with the approval.

4. How to implement it in ECMS

Mount (Task Approvals page)

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ai/components/approval-copilot.css">
<script src="/ai/shared/ai-gateway.js"></script>
<script src="/ai/components/approval-copilot.js"></script>

<ai-approval-copilot mode="bulk" table="#approvals"
     approver-limit="{{user.approvalLimit}}"></ai-approval-copilot>
<ai-approval-copilot mode="digest" table="#approvals"
     approver-limit="{{user.approvalLimit}}"></ai-approval-copilot>

<script>
document.addEventListener('ai-approval-preselect', e =>
  approvals.select(e.detail.ids));
document.addEventListener('ai-approval-decision', e =>
  approvals.decide(e.detail));   // host owns approvals
</script>

Build steps

  1. Expose the request data the checks need: amount, budget line balance, requester's advances, vendor, unit price.
  2. Encode the rules (limits, advances, split purchases, price outliers) as a rules service that uses the tenant's Asset Thresholds.
  3. Index POLICY_DOCUMENTS in Drive for retrieval, keeping each file's classification.
  4. Expose /ecms/approval-digest and /ecms/approval-bulk-screen on the CICOD-AI Gateway.
  5. Mount the digest panel and the bulk button. Wire the decision event to the existing approval API.
  6. Pilot with Admin & Supplies approvers. Track the override rate and cycle time for 6 weeks.

Controls

  • Feature flag ai.ecms.approvalCopilot in ECMS Settings, on/off per tenant. Safe bulk approve can be switched off separately.
  • Bulk screening only includes items with no failed or flagged checks, and a per-item cap (default ₦250,000) set by an admin.
  • Every digest, recommendation, override and bulk selection is written to the ECMS Audit Log (feature: CICOD-AI Approvals).
  • The digest only reads attachments and policy files the approver may open. CICOD-AI never approves: every decision needs the approver's click.
