Task Brief & Suggested Reply

This page shows how CICOD-AI enhances the ECMS Tasks screens. It adds a Task brief to summarize tasks and suggest actions, a Suggest reply feature for quick comments, and a ✦ preview button to view summaries directly from the task list.

Screens: /ecms/tasks?tab=all · /ecms/tasks/:id?tab=overviewComponent: <ai-task-brief>Endpoints: /ecms/task-brief · /ecms/suggest-reply

Act Now

Every row, count, name, comment, history entry, attachment and approval shown here comes from govtest (All Tasks and the task pages for 17578, 17573, 17572 and 17571, crawled 28 Sep 2026). The other rows open a task page built from their All Tasks row. Parts that weren't captured (description, comments, history, files) are marked as such. The form that Act Now opens, and the box that Add comment opens, weren't captured. The comment box shown here is a stand-in, built to hold the Suggest reply bar.

Exactly where it goes

① Task brief (main)The first block of the task's Overview tab, under the "Approval Required" banner and above Description. It builds when the task opens and rebuilds after each new comment. The Next best action chip lives here because it acts on this task.
② Suggest replyA bar on top of the Add comment box, on the Comments tab and from the Activity Feed. The draft goes into the box only when the officer clicks Insert. They then edit it and post it.
③ ✦ on All TasksA small button right after the Task ID in each row. Clicking it opens a preview of the same brief beside the row, with Open task →. Nothing else on the list changes.
UnchangedAct Now, Reassign, Change workflow, the tabs and Progress stay exactly as they are. Briefs are never built for all 15,399 open tasks, only when someone clicks ✦ or opens a task.

How it works

A summariser reads what ECMS already stores on the task: description, progress steps, approval, people, comments, history and attachments. Rules find the blocker and the ask. A writer model drafts the comment.

Capture

The task's fields from Overview, plus Comments, History (status and approval) and Attachments with their classification, are sent to /ecms/task-brief.

Protect

The gateway removes personal data (NIN, phone, email) and leaves out content the viewer can't open. OFFICIAL and SECRET files stay on the sovereign model.

Summarise

An LLM writes exactly five lines: request, progress, attachments, activity and priority or age.

Blocker & ask

Rules read the approval level (for example "Level 1 · Admin Officer · Pending"), missing assignees and silence since assignment, then work out what the viewer should do.

Draft

/ecms/suggest-reply writes the comment addressed to the right person, such as the approver or the assignee, in the chosen tone, using anything the officer has typed.

Confirm & learn

The officer inserts, edits or discards the draft and rates the brief. Every decision is sent to /feedback and the ECMS Audit Log.

Benefits to this feature

Counts are from govtest on 28 Sep 2026. Percentages are targets.

15,399 open

Triage from the list

The ✦ preview shows at a glance which open tasks aren't assigned or are stuck on approval. On the first page, 11 of 20 rows are waiting on an Admin Officer approval.

Approval stalls

Blockers named, not hunted

"Approval Required" today says what is blocked, not who needs to act. The brief names the level, role and alternate, and drafts the nudge.

−30 to 50%

Handling time per task

Target. There is no need to click through Overview, Comments, History and Attachments before acting.

−60%

Time to reply

Target. Comments start from a draft in the MDA's tone instead of a blank box.

How to implement it in ECMS

Three additions to existing screens

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ai/components/task-brief.css">
<script src="/ai/shared/ai-gateway.js"></script>
<script src="/ai/components/task-brief.js"></script>

<!-- Task JSON the page already loads, exposed once -->
<div id="task-src" hidden>
  <script type="application/json" data-task>{{task | json}}</script>
</div>

<!-- 1. /ecms/tasks/:id?tab=overview, first block -->
<ai-task-brief mode="brief" source="#task-src"
               viewer="{{user.fullName}}"></ai-task-brief>

<!-- 2. On top of the Add comment box -->
<ai-task-brief mode="reply" source="#task-src"
               intent="#comment"></ai-task-brief>

<!-- 3. /ecms/tasks?tab=all, after the Task ID -->
<button class="ai-brief-ic" data-task="{{task.id}}">✦</button>
<!-- on click, mount in a popover: -->
<ai-task-brief mode="brief" variant="preview" source="#row-src"></ai-task-brief>

<script>
document.addEventListener('ai-brief-open', e => router.push(taskUrl(e.detail.taskId)));
document.addEventListener('ai-task-action', e => e.detail.action === 'draft'
  ? comments.openComposer(e.detail.to) : people.openReassign());
document.addEventListener('ai-reply-insert', e => comments.setDraft(e.detail.text));
</script>

Controls

  • Feature flag ai.ecms.taskBrief in ECMS Settings, on/off per tenant. The list ✦ and Suggest reply can each be switched off on their own.
  • The next best action never runs on its own. It only opens Add comment or Reassign, and the officer finishes the action.
  • Every brief, draft and decision is written to the ECMS Audit Log (feature: CICOD-AI Task Brief).
  • Only comments, history and files the viewer is allowed to see are sent. Posted drafts are marked as CICOD-AI-assisted.
