2. How it works

1. Describe

Type what the form collects, or upload a photo or PDF of the paper form.

2. Build

CICOD-AI picks field types, options and Nigerian rules (NIN, phone, NUBAN).

3. Place

Fields go straight into the designer, with destination and email response set.

4. Check & save

Review, test with sample data, then Save. Your edits help it learn.

3. Benefits

1 hr → 5 min

Faster forms

A 15-field form in minutes, not field by field.

Paper → online

Same-day digitising

Photograph a paper form and publish it the same day.

Clean data

Checked at entry

Wrong NINs, phones and account numbers are caught as people type.

4. How to build it

Mount (Create Form page)

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ai/components/prompt-to-form.css">
<script src="/ai/shared/ai-gateway.js"></script>
<script src="/ai/components/prompt-to-form.js"></script>

<ai-form-builder max-description="1500"></ai-form-builder>

<script>
document.addEventListener('ai-form-apply', e => {
  const d = e.detail;           // host owns the form draft
  formDraft.set({ name: d.name, type: d.formType,
                  description: d.description });
  formDraft.setFields(d.fields);
});
</script>

Build steps

  1. Add /ecms/form/draft and /ecms/form/from-scan to the CICOD-AI Gateway.
  2. Add the button to Create Form and the designer. Place the returned fields on the canvas.
  3. Add the Rule setting to field cards. Pilot with 20 Registry paper forms.

Controls

  • On/off per tenant: ai.ecms.promptToForm.
  • Nothing is saved until the officer clicks Save. Unclear scan text is flagged for review.
  • Scans stay in-country. Every AI form is logged in the ECMS Audit Log.
