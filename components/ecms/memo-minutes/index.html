Minute Summary & Action Items

Get an instant overview of any memo file. The AI reads the entire chain of minutes to tell you who is holding the file, its current status, and any open queries. It automatically converts written instructions into trackable ECMS tasks with owners and due dates. You can preview this summary directly from the Memos list using the ✦ icon, or view the full summary inside the memo.

Exactly where it goes

① File summary (main)At the top of an opened memo, above the memo body and its minutes. It opens with ✦ Summarize File, or already expanded when you arrive from the list preview. It rebuilds when a new minute is added.
② ✦ on Memos & DraftsNext to the memo name, only on memos with 3 or more minutes. The preview shows the status, who holds the file and open actions, with Open with full summary or Open memo.
③ Action items → TasksCreate ECMS task on each action item sends the task to Tasks with the memo Ref. The new task shows under "Tasks from this file".
UnchangedThe tabs (All Memos, My Drafts, Draft Reviews), search, Custom View, Create and Minute work as they do today. Summaries are only built when someone clicks.

How it works

The AI reads through the history of a memo to find key actions, dates, and decisions automatically.

Capture

The AI reviews the minute text, dates, and the people involved to build a complete picture of the memo's history.

Protect

You only see summaries of minutes you are authorized to read. Highly sensitive memos are kept completely secure and private.

Understand

The system understands government shorthand (like "pls treat", "KIV", "for your action") and figures out if questions raised in earlier minutes have been answered yet.

Extract Actions

When someone writes an instruction, the AI turns it into an actionable item. It automatically figures out who should do the work and when it is due based on the text.

Create Tasks

Clicking "Create ECMS task" instantly moves that instruction into your official Tasks list so it can be tracked until completion.

Confirm & Learn

You always have the final say. You can change the assignee or due date before creating a task. Your feedback helps the system get even smarter.

Benefits

Designed to save time and ensure no instruction slips through the cracks.

How to implement it in ECMS

Two additions to existing screens

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ai/components/memo-minutes.css">
<script src="/ai/shared/ai-gateway.js"></script>
<script src="/ai/components/memo-minutes.js"></script>

<!-- 1. Opened memo: above the memo body -->
<ai-minute-summary source="#minutes" subject="{{memo.subject}}"
    ref="{{memo.refNo}}"></ai-minute-summary>
<li data-minute data-by="Permanent Secretary"
    data-to="Director, General Services" data-date="18/09/2026">…</li>

<!-- 2. Memos & Drafts: after the name, when minutes >= 3 -->
<button class="ai-memo-ic" data-memo="{{memo.id}}">✦</button>
<!-- on click, mount in a popover: -->
<ai-minute-summary variant="preview" source="#row-minutes"
    ref="{{memo.refNo}}"></ai-minute-summary>

<script>
on('ai-minute-open', e => router.push(memoUrl(e.detail.ref),
  { summary: e.detail.summary }));   // renders <ai-minute-summary open>
on('ai-minute-create-task', e => tasksApi.create({
  ...e.detail, links: [{ type: 'memo', ref: e.detail.ref }] }));
</script>

Build steps

  1. Build the shorthand dictionary (DFA, Perm Sec, HOD, KIV, "pls treat") with Registry officers, per MDA.
  2. Expose /ecms/memo/summarise-minutes on the CICOD-AI Gateway, returning structured JSON only.
  3. Mark minutes in the memo view with data-minute attributes and mount the card above the memo.
  4. Add the ✦ to Memos & Drafts rows with 3 or more minutes, and cache each summary until the next minute.
  5. Wire ai-minute-create-task to the Tasks API with a back-link to the memo.

Controls

  • Feature flag ai.ecms.memoMinutes in ECMS Settings, on/off per tenant. The list ✦ can be switched off on its own.
  • Minimum chain length before the ✦ and the card show (default 3 minutes).
  • Summaries and created tasks are written to the ECMS Audit Log (feature: CICOD-AI Minutes) with the memo Ref.
  • The card never shows minutes the viewer can't read. Tasks can only be assigned to roles the creator may assign to.
