Minute Summary & Action Items

A memo file can carry a long chain of minutes: "pls treat", queries, clarifications, "approved subject to availability of funds". This card reads the whole chain and tells a senior officer where the file stands: status, decisions, open queries and who is holding it. It also turns instructions such as "for your action by Friday" into action items with an assignee and due date, each one a click away from becoming a tracked ECMS task.

Screen: ECMS → Memos → Memo view / Draft ReviewsComponent: <ai-minute-summary>Endpoint: /ecms/memo/summarise-minutes

1. Where it goes: live demo

This is a memo opened from Memos & Drafts with eight minutes on it. The summary card at the top is generated from those minutes. Click Create ECMS task on an action item (change the assignee or due date first if you like) and it appears under Tasks from this file. Then add your own minute at the bottom, e.g. "Head Works: pls submit completion certificate by Friday", and click Re-summarise.

2. How it works

An LLM reads the minute chain in order. Rules pick out executive shorthand, dates and amounts, and ECMS task routing proposes the queue.

Capture

Each minute's author role, addressee role, date and text, plus the memo Ref No and subject, are sent to /ecms/memo/summarise-minutes.

Protect

Only minutes the viewer can read are sent. The gateway redacts personal data and keeps Confidential or Secret files on the sovereign model.

Understand

A shorthand dictionary maps "DFA", "Perm Sec", "pls treat", "KIV" and "for your action" to roles and intents. The LLM then writes the status, decisions and queries, and checks whether each query was answered by a later minute.

Extract actions

Instructions become action items. The assignee is the addressee or the role named in the sentence ("Director GS to ensure…"). The due date comes from "by Friday" or "within 48 hours", or defaults to 3 working days.

Create tasks

"Create ECMS task" sends the title, assignee, due date and memo Ref to Tasks, and Smart Routing picks the queue. The task links back to the memo.

Confirm & learn

Officers can change the assignee or date before creating. "Summary is right" or "Not accurate" and every edit go to /feedback and are used to tune the prompt and the shorthand list.

Targets from the CICOD-AI augmentation analysis and the CICOD business case ("decisions become tracked work").

Senior officer read time

0 → 100%

4. How to implement it in ECMS

Mount (Memo view)

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ai/components/memo-minutes.css">
<script src="/ai/shared/ai-gateway.js"></script>
<script src="/ai/components/memo-minutes.js"></script>

<ai-minute-summary source="#minutes"
    subject="Renovation of Kano Store warehouse roof"
    ref="CICOD/WKS/MEM/2026/118"></ai-minute-summary>

<!-- each minute -->
<li data-minute data-by="Permanent Secretary"
    data-to="Director, General Services"
    data-date="18/09/2026">…</li>

<script>
on('ai-minute-create-task', e => tasksApi.create({
  ...e.detail, links: [{ type: 'memo', ref: e.detail.ref }]
}));
</script>

Build steps

  1. Build the shorthand dictionary (DFA, Perm Sec, HOD, KIV, "pls treat") with Registry officers, per MDA.
  2. Expose /ecms/memo/summarise-minutes on the CICOD-AI Gateway, returning structured JSON only.
  3. Mark minutes in the memo view with data-minute attributes and mount the card above the memo.
  4. Wire ai-minute-create-task to the Tasks API with a back-link to the memo.
  5. Pilot in one directorate; compare the CICOD-AI's action list with what officers actually did.

Controls

  • Feature flag ai.ecms.memoMinutes in ECMS Settings, on/off per tenant.
  • Minimum chain length before the card shows (default 3 minutes).
  • Summaries and created tasks are written to the ECMS Audit Log (feature: CICOD-AI Minutes) with the memo Ref.
  • The card never shows minutes the viewer can't read; tasks can only be assigned to roles the creator may assign to.
