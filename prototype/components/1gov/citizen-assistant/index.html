Citizen Assistant

Today the CICOD Paperless Service Portal asks citizens to select from 19 technical queue names (such as Marine Casualty & Incident Investigation or Fraud and Risk Management) and manually fill complex forms. The Citizen Assistant offers conversational intake in plain English, Nigerian Pidgin, Hausa, Yorùbá, or Igbo. It identifies the target MDA desk, extracts incident dates and locations, prompts conversationally for only the missing details, accepts document or receipt uploads, issues an instant Tracking ID, and explains request milestones in plain language.

2. How it works

A zero-shot classifier matches citizen statements against CICOD's active MDA service catalog, while an entity extractor collects dates, locations, and references before asking for missing details.

Capture

The citizen describes their situation in plain language (or voice), or uploads a supporting receipt or photo to /portal/assist.

Protect

PII is securely handled within the secure enterprise cloud. Phone numbers and National Identity Numbers (NIN) are verified before submission.

Classify & extract

The model maps narrative text to the right service queue, extracting dates, locations, vessel names, and receipt numbers with confidence scoring.

Conversational intake

Instead of presenting a multi-page blank webform, the assistant asks only for the remaining missing fields one at a time.

Track & deflect

Common questions are answered immediately from the Service Charter, while tracking tokens offer plain-language progress summaries.

Confirm & learn

The citizen reviews all extracted details and confirms explicit data consent before submission, generating an audit-logged feedback event.

4. How to implement it in Paperless Portal

Mount (Paperless Service Portal)

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ai/components/citizen-assistant.css">
<script src="/ai/shared/ai-gateway.js"></script>
<script src="/ai/components/citizen-assistant.js"></script>

<ai-citizen-assistant
  queues="#queue-list"
  mda="CICOD"
  open>
</ai-citizen-assistant>

<script>
document.addEventListener('ai-citizen-queue', e => {
  portal.highlightQueue(e.detail.queue);
});
document.addEventListener('ai-citizen-submit', e => {
  portal.registerEngagement(e.detail);
});
document.addEventListener('ai-citizen-track', e => {
  portal.displayMilestones(e.detail);
});
</script>

Build steps

  1. Expose /portal/assist on the CICOD-AI Gateway with support for intake, tracking explanations, and Service Charter FAQ deflection.
  2. Connect active queue taxonomies and mandatory fields from ECMS Workflows into the assistant's schema registry.
  3. Index published MDA Service Charters and FAQs to enable real-time deflection before tickets are raised.
  4. Mount <ai-citizen-assistant> in the portal footer; link custom events to queue catalog highlights and tracking views.
  5. Deploy sovereign machine translation models for Hausa, Yorùbá, Igbo, and Nigerian Pidgin.

Controls

  • Feature flag ai.portal.citizenAssistant in Administration, controllable per portal and MDA tenant.
  • Classification confidence threshold (default 0.70); prompts user with a queue selector when below threshold.
  • Session auditing: all conversational intake interactions, tracking lookups, and queue overrides are logged.
  • Strict NDPR compliance: citizen consent is captured explicitly before personal data is stored in ECMS.
