Appraisal Copilot

Helps staff write SMART KPIs from a vague objective and drafts the self-appraisal comment from real evidence: completed ECMS tasks, approved memos and closed tickets, each cited. Supervisors get a consistency hint when a rating is higher or lower than the evidence supports. Nothing is saved until the person confirms it.

2. How it works

An LLM with a KPI library writes the goal, retrieval over the CICOD unified index finds the evidence, and a simple scoring rule compares ratings with that evidence.

Capture

The typed objective, the appraisee's role and department, and for drafts the KPI text, target and period are sent to the /pms/* endpoints.

Protect

The gateway removes personal data (NIN, phone, email). Appraisal content is Confidential by default, so it stays on the sovereign model. Evidence retrieval is trimmed to what the appraisee is allowed to see.

Write the KPI

An LLM rewrites the objective against a KPI library for the MDA. It names a real data source (ECMS queue, Drive folder, Asset store) so the KPI can be measured automatically.

Find evidence

Hybrid search across closed ECMS tasks, approved memos, Drive activity, Conference attendance and help-desk tickets in the period, ranked by match to each KPI.

Draft & check

The draft comment cites each evidence item as [1], [2]. The consistency check compares the self-rating with evidence strength and flags gaps of one point or more.

Confirm & learn

The appraisee edits and inserts, or discards. The supervisor notes or dismisses hints. Every decision goes to /feedback and improves the KPI library and ranking.

4. How to implement it in PMS

Mount (Appraisal page)

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/ai/components/pms-appraisal-copilot.css">
<script src="/ai/shared/ai-gateway.js"></script>
<script src="/ai/components/pms-appraisal-copilot.js"></script>

<ai-appraisal-copilot source="#appraisal-form"
    period="2026 Mid-Year Review"
    staff="Prince Ekpenyong"></ai-appraisal-copilot>

<script>
document.addEventListener('ai-kpi-apply', e => appraisal.addKpi(e.detail));
document.addEventListener('ai-appraisal-draft', e =>
  appraisal.setComment(e.detail.kpiId, e.detail.comment, e.detail.citations));
document.addEventListener('ai-consistency-result', e =>
  appraisal.showHints(e.detail.hints));
</script>

Build steps

  1. Get read access to the PMS appraisal and goal APIs and confirm the real field names (this page assumes KPI, target, weight, rating, comment).
  2. Build a KPI library per MDA from existing approved goals and the ECMS queue, Drive folder and Asset store catalogues.
  3. Expose /pms/smart-kpi, /pms/appraisal-draft and /pms/consistency on the CICOD-AI Gateway, using the unified index for evidence.
  4. Mount the component on the appraisal form and wire the three events to the form store.
  5. Pilot with one department for one review cycle. Track edit rate on drafts and supervisor agreement with hints.
  6. Roll out to Team Reviews for supervisors once hint accuracy is agreed with HR.

Controls

  • Feature flag ai.cicod.pmsAppraisalCopilot, on/off per tenant, with separate switches for drafts and supervisor hints.
  • Consistency hints appear only for a rating gap of 1 point or more. Hints are advisory and never change a rating.
  • Every suggestion, insert and dismissal is written to the PMS audit trail (feature: CICOD-AI Appraisal).
  • Evidence is permission-trimmed: only items the appraisee could open themselves. Secret files are never quoted, only counted.
